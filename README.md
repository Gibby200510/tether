# Tether

A focus and study app for college students: a focus timer with check-ins and white noise, distraction tracking, study notes and quizzes from screenshots, and step-by-step help with homework problems.

**Use it:** https://gibby200510.github.io/tether/

The AI features use Google's Gemini. Click **AI settings** in the app and paste your own free key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey. The key and all your data are saved only in your browser.
